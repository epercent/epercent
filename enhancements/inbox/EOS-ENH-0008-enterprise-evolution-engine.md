# Enhancement Summary

## Objective

Create the Enterprise Evolution Engine responsible for governing the complete lifecycle of enterprise improvements from idea through deployment and enterprise value realization.

## Responsibilities

- Monitor Enhancement Repository
- Discover improvement opportunities
- Prioritize enhancements
- Invoke Decision Intelligence
- Request Executive Approval
- Submit approved work to the Architecture Office
- Generate Capability Packages
- Assign work to the AI Workforce
- Monitor implementation
- Coordinate Validation
- Coordinate Release
- Assimilate Knowledge
- Update Enterprise Genome
- Measure Enterprise Value Created

## Inputs

- Executive ideas
- AI-generated recommendations
- User feedback
- Operational telemetry
- Technical debt
- Market intelligence
- Research
- Risk assessments
- Knowledge discoveries

## Outputs

- Capability Packages
- AI Workforce Assignments
- PMO updates
- Release recommendations
- Knowledge updates
- Enterprise Genome updates
- Enterprise Value reports

## Success Criteria

- Every enhancement follows a governed lifecycle.
- AI workforce assignments are automated.
- Human approvals occur where constitutionally required.
- Enterprise knowledge grows after every completed enhancement.
- Enterprise value created by enhancements is measurable.

## Dependencies

- Enhancement Repository
- Decision Intelligence Office
- Architecture Office
- AI Workforce Office
- Validation Office
- Release Office
- Knowledge Office
- Enterprise Value Office
- Mission Control

## Risks

- Excessive autonomous decision-making
- Poor prioritization
- Insufficient governance
- Lack of executive oversight

## Approval Notes

The Enterprise Evolution Engine must never bypass constitutional governance or required executive approvals.
