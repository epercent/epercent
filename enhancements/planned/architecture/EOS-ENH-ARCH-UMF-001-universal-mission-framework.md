# EOS Enhancement Submission

## Enhancement ID
EOS-ENH-ARCH-UMF-001

## Title
Universal Mission Framework

## Status
Planned

## Priority
High

## Category
Enterprise Architecture

## Owner Office
Enterprise Architecture Office

## Governing Office
CTO Office / AI Development Office

## Sprint Eligibility
Deferred — Post Autonomous Engineering MVP

## Purpose
Create a Universal Mission Framework capable of orchestrating Engineering, Intelligence, Capital, Commercial, Knowledge, Digital Twin Asset, Swarm, Recovery, and future enterprise missions through a common lifecycle.

## Business Problem
Mission concepts are emerging independently across EOS. Without a universal abstraction, different offices may create incompatible execution models, increasing architectural complexity and future refactoring.

## Proposed Enhancement
Introduce a Universal Mission Object and lifecycle that can be used by all Coordination Engines.

The framework should include:

- Universal Mission Object
- Mission Types
- Mission Lifecycle
- Capability Requirements
- Workforce Assignment
- Coordination Engine Integration
- Governance Gates
- Audit Trail
- Knowledge Capture
- Enterprise Value Measurement

## Dependencies
- Engineering Coordination Engine
- AI Development Orchestrator
- AI Provider Gateway
- Coordination Engine Framework
- First autonomous engineering workflow

## Implementation Trigger
Begin after EOS successfully executes its first autonomous engineering workflow.

## Expected Enterprise Impact
- Single enterprise execution model
- Reduced technical debt
- Consistent governance
- Unified audit trail
- Enterprise-wide orchestration
- Higher Autonomous Development Index
- Foundation for autonomous enterprise operations

## Notes
Captured during Sprint 4 to avoid scope drift. Do not implement until the current AI Development Orchestrator and AI Provider Gateway objectives are complete.
