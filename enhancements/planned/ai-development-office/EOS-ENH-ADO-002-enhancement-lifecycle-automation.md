# EOS Enhancement Submission

## Enhancement ID
EOS-ENH-ADO-002

## Title
Enhancement Lifecycle Automation

## Status
Planned

## Priority
Critical

## Category
AI Development Office

## Owner Office
AI Development Office

## Governing Office
CTO Office

## Sprint Eligibility
Deferred — After Build Queue, Assignment Engine, Engineering Ledger, and AI Provider Gateway

## Purpose
Enable the AI Development Office to continuously monitor the Enhancement Repository, determine build readiness, estimate effort, estimate enterprise value, recommend sprint priorities, and prepare engineering missions for human approval.

## Business Problem
Enhancements are currently captured manually and require human review to determine readiness, priority, dependencies, and implementation sequence. This limits the speed at which EOS can transition toward AI-managed development.

## Proposed Enhancement
Create an automated enhancement lifecycle process that can:

- Scan the Enhancement Repository
- Classify enhancements by category, priority, dependency, and readiness
- Estimate engineering effort
- Estimate commercial and investor impact
- Identify blocked and build-ready enhancements
- Recommend sprint candidates
- Generate implementation plans
- Convert approved enhancements into engineering missions
- Update Mission Control with enhancement status

## Dependencies
- Enhancement Repository
- Engineering Coordination Engine
- Build Queue
- Assignment Engine
- Engineering Ledger
- AI Provider Gateway

## Expected Outcome
EOS begins generating its own engineering backlog from approved enhancements.

## Expected Enterprise Impact
- Faster sprint planning
- Better enhancement governance
- Reduced human coordination burden
- Higher Autonomous Development Index
- Clearer path toward AI-led development
- Stronger investor demonstration of self-improving enterprise software

Captured during Sprint 4 to preserve strategic direction without interrupting the current sprint.
