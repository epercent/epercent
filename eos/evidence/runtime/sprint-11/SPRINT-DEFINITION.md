## Sprint Objective

Establish the canonical human authority, governance, supervision,
autonomy-policy, safety and intervention control plane required to govern EOS
as it progresses from governed self-build toward materially higher autonomous
operation.

## Separation of Responsibilities

### Enterprise Control

Enterprise Control is the authority and intervention layer.

It will ultimately provide governed capabilities for:

- approval;
- rejection;
- suspension;
- freeze;
- resume;
- override;
- autonomy-level configuration;
- protected-domain policy;
- permission boundaries;
- blast-radius limits;
- disruption and resource limits;
- rollback authority;
- emergency controls;
- inspection of autonomous change provenance.

Existing Enterprise Control summary/runtime surfaces must be reused where
appropriate rather than replaced.

### Mission Control

Mission Control is the operational supervision and visibility layer.

It observes and exposes:

- mission state;
- agent state;
- lifecycle state;
- Engineering Ledger state;
- self-improvement synchronization;
- governance state;
- blocking conditions;
- exceptions;
- attention requirements;
- operational progress.

Mission Control must not become the ultimate human authority layer.

### AEAF

AEAF is the canonical autonomy authority framework.

The intended levels are:

A0 — Observe
A1 — Assisted
A2 — Governed Autonomous
A3 — Enterprise Autonomous
A4 — Swarm Autonomous

Autonomy must be configurable by governed domain and must not be inferred
solely from runtime capability.

A3 and A4 must remain gated until the Governance Control Plane proves that
higher autonomy can be constrained, observed, stopped, audited and reversed.

### EOS Runtime / iiAgents / Self-Build

The existing governed autonomous engineering runtime remains singular.

Sprint 11 must not create a second autonomous engineering runtime.

The governed Enhancement → Self-Build lifecycle established by Sprint 10.6
remains authoritative.

### Engineering Ledger

The Engineering Ledger remains the canonical engineering lifecycle record.

Sprint 11 must consume and expose it rather than duplicate its authority.

### Self-Evolution Register

A permanent Self-Evolution / Autonomous Change Register is required for every
self-generated EOS change.

It must ultimately preserve:

- initiating source;
- initiating agent/system;
- rationale;
- affected components;
- authority;
- autonomy level;
- risk classification;
- tests;
- verification evidence;
- approval evidence;
- version;
- rollback point;
- outcome;
- provenance;
- Engineering Ledger linkage;
- Enterprise Memory linkage.

Objective 11.1 defines this requirement only.

Implementation occurs in later Sprint 11 objectives.

### Recovery / Rollback

Sprint 11 must reuse the verified continuity, recovery, known-good-state,
rollback and resilience infrastructure already established in Sprint 10.x.

It must not create a separate recovery authority.

## Architectural Rules

1. Human / enterprise authority remains supreme at defined governance boundaries.

2. Enterprise Control governs authority.

3. Mission Control governs operational visibility and coordination.

4. AEAF governs permissible autonomy.

5. The existing autonomous engineering runtime remains singular.

6. Closure of an autonomous activity does not grant unrestricted continuation.

7. Higher autonomy must be explicitly authorized.

8. Every self-generated EOS change must be reconstructable from evidence.

9. Emergency freeze and rollback must remain available above autonomous execution.

10. Governance authority must not be manufactured by the autonomous runtime.

11. Existing Sprint 10.x recovery capability must be reused.

12. Existing Sprint 10.6 governed self-build capability must be reused.

13. Existing Enterprise Control and Mission Control implementations must be
    extended rather than duplicated.

14. A3 and A4 operation remain gated until Objective 11.8 is formally closed.
