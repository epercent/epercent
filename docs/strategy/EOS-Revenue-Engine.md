# EOS Revenue Engine

Version: 0.21.0

Capability: EOS-CAP-0029

## Purpose

The Revenue Engine turns EOS strategy into a portfolio of commercial opportunities with status, maturity, market potential, linked capabilities, and dependencies.

## Current State

The Revenue Engine is a foundation model. It does not perform financial forecasting, billing, sales automation, or audited valuation.

## Required Next Steps

- Define pricing assumptions.
- Link revenue streams to customer segments.
- Add opportunity pipeline records.
- Add governed write APIs for revenue updates.
- Validate revenue assumptions through customer discovery.
- Connect DTA lifecycle stages to commercial offers.
