## Purpose

The EOS investment thesis is now represented as live platform data. Mission Control should demonstrate the thesis through operational records, not only external presentation material.

## Thesis Elements

- Enterprise Objects as self-reporting operational entities.
- AI Workforce as governed executive and operational roles.
- Digital Twin Assets as enterprise models that can be monitored, improved, valued, and commercialized.
- Second Balance Sheet as a framework for recognizing digital operational assets.
- KIPR as the knowledge, intellectual property, patents, and research foundation.
- DTAP, DTAM, and DTAX as the platform, management, and exchange roadmap.
- Revenue Engine as the commercialization model.
