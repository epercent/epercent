# EOS KIPR

Version: 0.21.0

Capability: EOS-CAP-0029

KIPR means Knowledge, Intellectual Property, Patents, and Research.

## Areas

- Knowledge Assets
- White Papers
- Academic Papers
- Patents
- Research Projects
- Investor Briefs
- Conference Papers
- Doctoral Research
- Publications
- Commercial IP
- Future Books

## Purpose

KIPR extends the Agent Knowledge Repository into the publication, patent, commercial IP, investor, and doctoral research layer of EOS.

## Operating Rule

Every important strategic claim should link to KIPR evidence, including documents, research notes, investor briefs, publication drafts, patent candidates, Enterprise Objects, Digital Twin Assets, workflows, and executive owners.
