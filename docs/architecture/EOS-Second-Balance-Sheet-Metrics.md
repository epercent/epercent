# EOS Second Balance Sheet Metrics

Version: 0.18.0
Capability: EOS-CAP-0026
Status: Draft Internal Methodology

## Purpose

The Second Balance Sheet metrics layer tracks digital operating assets that may contribute to enterprise value but are not represented in a traditional financial balance sheet.

## Initial Metrics

- Knowledge Assets
- Agent Assets
- Live Enterprise Objects
- Digital Twin Assets
- Research Assets
- Workflow Assets
- Opportunity Assets
- Governance Assets
- Estimated Digital Asset Value
- Confidence Level
- Methodology Status

## Initial Status

The first EOS Second Balance Sheet is a draft internal methodology. It is designed for governance alignment, research development, and future investor narrative preparation.

## Important Limitation

All values are internal estimates only. They are not financial advice, not audited valuation, and not suitable for tax, accounting, securities, or investment decisions.
