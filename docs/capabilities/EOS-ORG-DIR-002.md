# EOS-ORG-DIR-002: EOS Executive Leadership Team

## Purpose

EOS-ORG-DIR-002 establishes the EOS Executive Leadership Team as an organizational model. It does not add AI functionality.

## Reporting Line

Every executive agent reports to the Chief Technology Officer.

## Executive Agents

| Agent ID | Name | Role | Department |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EOS-AGENT-CODEX | Codex | Chief Engineering Officer | Engineering |
| EOS-AGENT-HERMES | Hermes | Chief Knowledge Officer | Knowledge |
| EOS-AGENT-ATHENA | Athena | Chief Research Officer | Research |
| EOS-AGENT-ATLAS | Atlas | Chief Enterprise Architect | Architecture |
| EOS-AGENT-MERCURY | Mercury | Chief Opportunity Officer | Opportunity |
| EOS-AGENT-ARGUS | Argus | Chief Operations Officer | Operations |
| EOS-AGENT-VULCAN | Vulcan | Chief Quality Officer | Quality |

## Responsibilities

Hermes owns Knowledge Vault, Genesis, Enterprise Objects, documentation, governance, version history, and change management.

Athena owns research, white papers, academic publications, doctoral support, patents, Business Impact Assessments, and the research roadmap.

Atlas owns architecture, capability dependencies, technical debt, scalability, platform health, and architecture reviews.

Mercury owns market intelligence, enterprise discovery, opportunity scanning, news monitoring, and competitive analysis.

Argus owns monitoring, telemetry, platform health, alerts, and operations.

Vulcan owns QA, testing, security, performance, and compliance.

Codex owns software engineering, platform development, code quality, testing, build verification, refactoring, workspace automation, release engineering, engineering estimates, and ECCR production.

## Enterprise Object Model

EOS-ORG-DIR-002 creates:

- `EOS-ORG-DIR-002`
- `EOS-EXEC-LEADERSHIP-TEAM`
- `EOS-AGENT-MERCURY`
- `EOS-AGENT-ARGUS`
- `EOS-AGENT-VULCAN`

It also enriches existing executive agents with department, CTO reporting line, responsibilities, and executive metadata.
