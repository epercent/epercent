## Purpose

Enterprise Intelligence Scenarios define reusable enterprise situations that EOS can understand, analyze, demonstrate, build, govern, and commercialize.

Each scenario is intended to serve as:

- a use case
- a sales story
- a demo scenario
- an architecture driver
- a capability package seed
- an AI workforce training object
- a Decision Intelligence test case
- a Digital Twin Asset opportunity
- a Second Balance Sheet input
- a commercial value narrative
