# EOS Constitution

Constitution ID: EOS-CON-001

Version: 0.1

Status: Foundational

---

## Article I

### Purpose

Executive Operating System (EOS) exists to discover, create, govern, operate, and continuously improve enterprise value through intelligence, knowledge, Digital Twin Assets, AI Workforce Operations, and governed decision making.

---

## Article II

### Mission

Transform ideas into governed enterprise value.

---

## Article III

### Guiding Principles

1. Enterprise before software.
2. Knowledge before implementation.
3. Governance before automation.
4. Enterprise Objects before documents.
5. Intelligence before execution.
6. Human oversight for strategic decisions.
7. Continuous enterprise evolution.
8. Every improvement creates enterprise value.

---

## Article IV

### Constitutional Assets

EOS recognizes the following constitutional assets:

- Kernel
- Genome
- Enterprise Object Model
- Enterprise Build System
- Knowledge System
- AI Workforce
- Decision Intelligence
- Mission Control
- Second Balance Sheet

---

## Article V

### Constitutional Offices

The enterprise shall operate through constitutional offices governed by Executive Council authority.

---

## Article VI

### Enterprise Evolution

EOS shall continuously improve itself through governed Enhancement Objects evaluated by Decision Intelligence and executed by the Enterprise Build System.
