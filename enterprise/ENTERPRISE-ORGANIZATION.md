# EOS Enterprise Organization

Version: 0.1

## Enterprise

Executive Operating System (EOS)

The enterprise is organized into constitutional offices responsible for governing every aspect of enterprise operation, evolution, intelligence, knowledge, and value creation.

## Primary Offices

1. Executive Council
2. Office of the Chief Executive Officer
3. Office of the Chief Technology Officer
4. Architecture Office
5. Program Management Office
6. Decision Intelligence Office
7. AI Workforce Office
8. Validation Office
9. Release Office
10. Knowledge Office
11. Enterprise Value Office

## Constitutional Principle

Every capability, enterprise object, enhancement, workflow, project, and AI agent belongs to one governing office.

No enterprise object exists without ownership.
